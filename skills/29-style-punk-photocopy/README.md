## The style

Your video turns into a cut-and-paste gig flyer. The opening steps back into a stack of copies while fat yellow brush strokes are painted and wiped off. Torn yellow paper sweeps across the cuts. On a key beat the picture becomes a black speckled poster with a yellow-edged speech-bubble window and tilted black label strips, and white deckle-edged photo cards slide in showing your other shots. Every caption sits on a hard black bar in sturdy slab serif: white words, with one big yellow keyword.

Perfect for music, culture, youth, street, fashion, event and rebellious "say it loud" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every stroke, wipe, card and poster, exact caption lines with times and yellow words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan lanes and card sides that keep your face clear, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, bars, bubble, cards, typography, motion) and 11 moments measured from a reference video, from the brush-strike opener to the end hold.
