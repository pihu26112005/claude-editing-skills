## The style
Your footage fills the whole frame and plays live, with plain cuts and no effects. Near the start, a short white italic serif title tilts gently upwards, rising in over the top of the picture. Captions build word by word in the lower middle: ordinary words in white serif, important words in yellow italic, each important word stepping down to a new line like a staircase. When a shot is bright or busy, the caption switches to black serif text on a clean white label near the top, and it changes in the same frame as the cut.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.
