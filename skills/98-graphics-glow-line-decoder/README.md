## The graphic

On a black screen a glowing headline types on in two lines. Six blurry placeholder lines appear below it and flip into grey pixel mosaics. The camera then zooms in hard and scrolls down the list while each mosaic line sharpens into readable glowing text, one after another.

Perfect for step lists, key-point hooks, checklists and "six things to know" intros.

**Give it:**
- a two-line headline (up to 24 characters per line);
- 6 short list lines (up to 14 characters each);
- optional: headline colour (default yellow), list colour (default white), background (default black).
