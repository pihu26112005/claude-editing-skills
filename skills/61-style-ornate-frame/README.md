## The style

Your video turns into a page from an antique storybook. The footage settles into a tall pointed-arch window on warm ivory paper while a fine gold frame draws itself around it, with a dotted line, curling vines, green leaves and deep red rosettes in the corners. A small black plate at the top unfolds with a gold small-caps title, and on lists its medallion flips to a red Roman numeral. Captions appear word by word in soft ivory serif on a dark plate below, with one golden word per line. A red scroll ribbon unfurls over a key number or phrase, and a soft glint of light sweeps over the gold on every cut.

Perfect for storytelling, history, books, heritage brands, luxury and "once upon a time" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a frame-and-typography video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, the frame and plates described in plain words, exact caption lines with times and gold words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear of the arch and plates, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, window and frame geometry, ornaments, typography, motion) and 13 moments, from the frame settle to the poster.
