## The graphic

One card slides up onto an empty table, then fans out into five. The camera tilts down to table level and slowly tours the cards. Three captions type onto the table, one under each outer card, as the camera pans and then pulls back to show the whole row.

Perfect for three-step explainers, chapter intros, tarot-style reveals and "pick a card" topics.
