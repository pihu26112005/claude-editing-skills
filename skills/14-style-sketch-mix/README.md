## The style

A sketchbook mixed into a reel. On the talking head, one fat white keyword sits inside a loose double pencil ellipse that draws on and wipes away, with handwritten captions arriving one word at a time. When the video cuts to B-roll, the speaker pops into a round bubble in the top corner and keeps talking under bold white captions. A cream paper page with a hand-circled phrase makes a calm beat on a person-free shot.

Motion is short and drawn: strokes draw on in a quarter of a second, text wipes in from the left, words fade in as they are spoken. The bubble is the only thing with a small overshoot.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every moment with its timing, all keywords and caption lines, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan a beat for every cut, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, shapes, typography, motion) and 9 moments measured from a reference video, from the scribble keyword to the scribble poster.
