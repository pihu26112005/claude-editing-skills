## The graphic

A bold centre phrase slides in letter by letter with a motion blur and a soft glow, on a dark hand-drawn grid with drifting dust. Four crayon arrows then grow from it one after another, to the top-left, top-right, bottom-left and bottom-right. At each arrow tip a condensed, glowing label pops in with a small overshoot while the camera slowly pushes in.

Perfect for keyword maps, topic breakdowns, four-point explainers and branching title intros.
