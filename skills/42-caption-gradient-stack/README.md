## The style

A bold, modern caption style. Each phrase is a short line of small white words that fade in one by one with a soft shadow, and under it one giant extra-bold keyword comes into focus out of a blur. The keyword is a different deep colour every time, bright on its left edge and fading darker to the right. Phrases zigzag between the top and the middle of the frame and fade out as the next one arrives. The footage is never touched.

Perfect for talking-head explainers, education, finance, real estate and brand videos that want the key word of every sentence to pop.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds this caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|

| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every caption chunk with its lane, keyword and colour, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |
