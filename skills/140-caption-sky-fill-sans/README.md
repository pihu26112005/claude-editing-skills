## The style

A bold caption style for talking-head and B-roll videos. Each phrase becomes a few small white sans words that fade in one by one, around one huge, extra-heavy keyword whose letters are filled with a soft cloud-and-sky texture that drifts slowly through them. The keyword sits across the chest (or in a top or low lane when the middle is busy), always clear of the face. Chunks never overlap, text always stays inside the frame, and the footage is never touched.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every caption line with its keyword, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, pick a lane that keeps your face clear, choose keywords, and build without overlaps or clipped text.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, lanes, typography, motion) and 7 moments, from the mid-frame sky keyword to the closing keyword.
