## The style

Your video plays full frame under bold white captions that come into focus word by word, with one key word dropped into a golden label box. On list steps the picture cuts to a warm cream paper page: your footage keeps playing in a rounded card, dark plum words stack beside it, and a big step number rolls up over a gradient progress bar. A hand-traced scalloped badge and small drawn stickers punctuate time phrases and payoffs.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a pages-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: scene map with timestamps, every page and badge, exact caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep faces and margins safe, find hook points and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, card, bar, typography, motion) and 12 moments, from the focus-in caption to the label closer.
