## The graphic

Five image cards sit in a row on a dark square panel, seen in perspective so the right side is nearer. Each card has a white knob on a drawn-on accent line and a label below. The camera starts far away, swoops in to the first card, then glides card by card to the fifth and settles.

Perfect for step-by-step processes, roadmaps, five-stage timelines, recipes and onboarding flows.

**Give it:**
- 5 short step labels (up to 14 characters each);
- 5 images (any photos; they are cropped to 5:3 and the subject stays in frame);
- optional: accent colour (default red-orange), panel and backdrop colours, label colour.
