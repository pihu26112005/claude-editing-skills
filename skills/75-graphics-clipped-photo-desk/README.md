# 75 · Clipped Photo Desk

## Results

**Your content, animated by Claude.** A dotted-paper desk gets a clipped photo, a colour caption strip and a paper label; the demo uses a "Farm To Cup" coffee-farm story with a generated dusk landscape.

![Clipped Photo Desk demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pihu26112005/motion-graphics-skills-media/main/gifs/75-graphics-clipped-photo-desk.gif)

**Format:** 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 6 s

## The graphic

A grey dotted desk, seen from above, starts empty. The camera eases in while a black pen, a coloured pen and a paper card slide in from the edges. A stack lands from the left: a card, a bright caption strip, a paper tape label and a metal paper clip. A tilted photo then slides in from the right under the strip. Two lines of text type on, letter by letter with a soft blur, one on the tape and one on the strip. Everything then holds still.

Perfect for reel intros, chapter titles, story covers and "photo with a headline" openers.

**Give it:**
- strip text (up to 18 characters);
- label text (up to 14 characters);
- 1 photo (any photo; it is cropped to a wide frame and the subject stays whole);
- optional: strip colour (default lime yellow), card colour (default blue-grey), pen colour (default red), text colour, dot grid off.

**What you get back:** a finished 9:16 video (or a still image), rendered and checked by Claude.
