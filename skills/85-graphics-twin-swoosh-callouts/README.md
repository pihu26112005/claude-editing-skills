# 85 · Twin Swoosh Callouts

## Results

**Your content, animated by Claude.** A glowing portrait card with two glowing brush swooshes that end in labels; the demo uses a night-hike scene with the labels "Trailhead" and "Summit" in aqua.

## The graphic

A tall rounded image card with a glowing border whips in from the left on a dark stage. The camera then pushes in and drifts right. Two tapered brush swooshes draw out from the card, each fading from a deep tail colour to a bright head, and a short glowing label types in at the tip of each. The camera eases back to show the whole composition and holds.

Perfect for app and product callouts, photo annotations, two-point explainers and neon-style highlights.

**Give it:**
- 1 portrait image (any photo or illustration; the subject stays whole);
- 2 short labels (up to 18 characters each);
- optional: swoosh colours, label colour, glow colour, card border colour, stage colour (defaults warm amber and yellow on near-black).

**What you get back:** a finished 16:9 video (or a still image), rendered and checked by Claude.
