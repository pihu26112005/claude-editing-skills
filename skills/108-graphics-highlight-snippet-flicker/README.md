## The graphic

Crumpled paper fills the frame. A keyword sits in a marker highlight at the same spot while the text around it cuts, about ten times a second, between nine article snippets: headlines, a byline page, a subscribe bar, faint body copy. Lines run off the edges with streaked smears, and the lower text is blurred as if scrolled fast.

Perfect for "everyone is talking about it" intros, trend teasers, brand-mention montages and hooks.
