## The style

A loud, flat poster look in five colours: hot orange, cobalt, mustard, cream and ink. A tall cream banner stamps in letter by letter at the top, then slides away. Captions build word by word on cream label bars, the newest word glowing orange. Cuts drop your footage into black-edged cards whose long shadow snaps back, stacked on an orange or mustard field. Now and then a curtain of circles and bars falls over the frame and lands on a blue wall of stars and words, then lifts again.

Perfect for food, lifestyle, finance, explainers and any talking-head reel that wants poster energy.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: scene map with timestamps, every graphic and its words, caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear, find hook points and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, cards, type, motion) and 11 moments measured from a reference video, from the banner stamp to the card grow.
