## The graphic

One post slot stays fixed in the middle of a pale square. For each word of your sentence it flickers rapidly through several different users' posts, so the avatar, name and like count keep changing while the word stays put. Faded posts above and below blur past, then the next word takes over. The last word holds.

Perfect for punchy sentence reveals, crowd-agrees gags, slogans, hooks and short-form text jokes.

**Give it:**
- a sentence of exactly 6 words (each up to 10 characters);
- optional: invented handles, avatar images, filler lines for neighbouring posts, accent, background and text colours (defaults: generated handles and avatars, blue accent, near-white background).
