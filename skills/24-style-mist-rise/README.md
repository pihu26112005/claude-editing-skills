## The style

Your video turns into a calm, elegant editorial piece. Captions are white serif words that fade in softly, one at a time, as you say them, with the important word in italic. Shots do not cut hard: they melt into each other with long, gentle dissolves, or the next shot lifts into view from the bottom like mist. On the big line, you glide upward while a soft, blurred band of your own B-roll rises over the lower half and the captions float on it. No boxes, no colours, no stickers, only light and type.

Perfect for coaching, personal brand, wellness, finance and "thoughtful talk" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt for the whole video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, the exact caption lines with times and italic words, how each dissolve and the rising band should look, and the things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear of the captions and the lift, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (ink, shadow, serif type, motion) and 13 moments, from the word-fade captions to the mist rise split.
