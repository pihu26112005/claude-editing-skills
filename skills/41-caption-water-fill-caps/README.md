## The style

A punchy caption style for talking videos. Small, ultra-bold white words pop up one by one just under the speaker's chin, and each phrase lands on one giant keyword that spans almost the full width, filled with a cool pale-to-deep blue water texture. The footage stays full frame and untouched; phrases change with hard cuts and reset on every shot cut. It is a caption style only, so no cards, wipes or backgrounds are added.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds this caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this caption style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every caption line with times and its giant keyword, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear, chunk your words into lead-in plus keyword, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, water fill, typography, motion) and 7 moments, from the lead-in plus giant keyword caption to the end hold.
