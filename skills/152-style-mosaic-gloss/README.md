## The style

A bubbly, glossy pastel look. Your clip sits in one big rounded card on a soft gradient page with faint grid lines and drifting light streaks, or runs full frame under cartoon clouds. Every cut is hidden by a pixel-mosaic wipe. Captions are tiny white whisper words with one big gradient keyword that types in with a glow, a glossy pill slot with a sliding ball knob for list items, and a rounded speech bubble for the payoff. Small frosted icon tiles pop in on the dark fade at the bottom.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds an interface-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: scene map with timestamps, every graphic and what it says, caption lines with times and keywords, allowed words and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep faces clear, find hook points and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, card, pill, bubble, typography, motion) and 12 moments, from the full-bleed open to the bubble closer.
