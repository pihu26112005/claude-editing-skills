## The style

Bold and graphic. A hot orange-red page, footage in sharp cards with a hard black block shadow, and two orange bands that carry a giant narrow cream title, letter by letter, over the full-frame speaker. Captions are small cream label boxes that grow word by word, with the newest word flashing orange. On a cut, two cards drop in with a shadow that snaps tight; a black slanted wedge sweeps the screen to a saffron-yellow single card.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every layout change, what each card shows, all titles, exact caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan a layout for every shot, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, cards, bands, typography, motion) and 10 moments measured from a reference video, from the band opener to the closing title bands.
