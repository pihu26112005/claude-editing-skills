## The graphic

Fifteen numbered rounded tiles slide in on a tilt over a dark, softly lit stage, then flatten into an overlapping row. A hand cursor rises, glides along the row, hovers tiles one by one and clicks the one you chose. That tile lifts out, the rest of the strip fans away to both sides, and the chosen tile grows to the centre and holds.

Perfect for "pick the best clip" reveals, template or recipe choosers, episode and chapter selectors, and app-style gallery intros.

**Give it:**
