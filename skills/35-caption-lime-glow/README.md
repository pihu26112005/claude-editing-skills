## The style

A caption style with a neon edge. Your words pop in two lines: clean white semibold words on top, and the one word that matters underneath in heavy slanted lime green with a soft glow. A gentle dark fade at the bottom keeps everything readable on any shot. On the topic word and the payoff, a giant outlined slanted title with a faint red and blue fringe slides across the top of the frame, or stacks into an echo. The footage itself is never touched.

Perfect for fitness, wearable tech, gadgets, creator tips and any fast, energetic talking-head reel.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every caption line with its time and lime keyword, the title moments, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear, chunk captions, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, type, motion, safe zones) and 10 moments, from the split-line caption to the closer keyword hold.
