## The graphic

Aged grid paper with dark corners and a slow flicker. A coloured date tag fades in, the headline appears word by word, and the detail text types underneath. A cut-out portrait rises from the bottom-right corner. A thin rule draws across and a blackletter masthead line types beside it. Finally a marker highlight sweeps over the headline, then over a phrase in the detail text.

Perfect for story intros, headline cards, vintage explainers, profile openers and announcement reels.

**Give it:**
- a date tag (up to 14 characters), a headline (up to 70 characters), a detail text (up to 170 characters) and a masthead line (up to 22 characters);
- a portrait (a cut-out on transparent background is best; faces stay whole);
- optional: highlight phrase, tag colour (default red), highlight colour (default yellow), text colour, paper colour, grid off.

**What you get back:** a finished 16:9 video (or a still image), rendered and checked by Claude.
