## The style

Your video gets a friendly hand drawing on it. A white pen line wobbles around you like it is redrawn every frame, and tiny doodles (a pink heart, music notes, a swirl, sparkles) pop up beside you while one of your words is handwritten next to your head. Small cream and pink paper tags with your key phrases stick onto the scene one by one, slightly tilted. On B-roll a sketchy white frame draws itself around the shot. On the payoff, a soft pink bar chart grows behind you while a little label with an arrow sits beside you. Captions are big handwritten white words with the key word on a pink marker stripe.

Perfect for creator tips, money and productivity advice, tutorials, lifestyle and "my way of doing it" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a doodle-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every doodle, tag and chart and what it says, exact caption lines with times and pink words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan zones that keep your face clear, check the person matte, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, boiling pen line, paper tags, typography, motion) and 8 moments, from the boil outline to the chart grow.
