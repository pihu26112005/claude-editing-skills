## The style

Your video turns into a soft, glassy app screen. It opens by shrinking into a rounded frosted card on a sky-blue gradient, with your topic in bold italic capitals at the bottom. On every cut the card glides into a new layout: a bright white stage with pink and blue diagonal bars, your shot in a glass card on top and a still of the neighbouring shot below. Captions sit in small frosted chips with one bold slanted keyword over light words. On the last line a thin blue-to-violet outline grows around you.

Perfect for finance, tech, lifestyle and explainer videos that want a calm, modern, soft-glass feel.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a graphics-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, the layout of every card, exact caption lines with times and keywords, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan layouts that keep your face clear, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, glass cards, stage, chips, typography, motion) and 11 moments measured from a reference video, from the glass card opener to the face outline closer.
