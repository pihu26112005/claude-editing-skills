## The style

A refined caption style for talking-head and B-roll videos. Each phrase becomes a tiny white serif line that fades in word by word, topped or followed by one huge, heavy, high-contrast serif keyword in a soft two-tone metallic gradient. When there is room above the speaker, the keyword rises just behind the top of the head so the person stands in front of it; otherwise it sits across the chest or in a lower lane. Chunks never overlap, text always stays inside the frame and off the face, and the footage is never touched.

**Give it:**

- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.
