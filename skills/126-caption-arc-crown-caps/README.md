## The style

A bold caption style for talking videos. The one word that matters in each phrase blurs in as a chunky accent-colour arch with a small 3D edge, curving like a crown above the speaker's head. The little words around it pop in one by one as tilted white capitals that pile up on the chest, and a punchline can land as one huge white word. The video stays full-frame and untouched; there are no boxes, cards or effects on the footage, and captions never touch the face.

Perfect for tutorials, explainers, finance, product and creator talking-head reels.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.
