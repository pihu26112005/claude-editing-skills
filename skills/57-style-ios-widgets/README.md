## The style

Your video turns into a phone home screen. Frosted glass widgets drop into the top corners with a step counter and a progress bar that fills as you talk. Each point you make lands as a white pill, a mouse cursor glides over and clicks it, and it snaps to system blue with a tick. The final line sits in a soft glass capsule, and a white profile card rises with your photo and a Follow button that gets clicked. Captions are bold white lines with one word in a blue pill.

Perfect for creator, tech, tips, tutorial and "follow for more" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds an interface-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every widget, pill and card with its words, caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep faces clear, find hook points and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, glass recipe, pills, card, typography, motion) and 9 moments, from the widget drop to the follow card.
