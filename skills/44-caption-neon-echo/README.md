## The style

A caption style with a night-city glow. Small bold white words pop in near your chest, one key word per phrase turns lime italic with a green halo, and one payoff word can explode into a giant scan-lined neon slab. An outline title slides across the top, and on the closing line the topic word repeats in outlined rows behind you with a faint red and cyan split. The footage stays full frame, untouched.

Perfect for hooks, tips, creator talks and punchy one-message reels.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every caption line with times and lime words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear, find hook points and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, type, motion, safe zones) and 9 moments measured from a reference video, from the pop caption to the echo stack.
