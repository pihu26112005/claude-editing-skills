## The style

A friendly, handmade caption style for talking-head and B-roll videos. Each phrase is one low line of small white brush-lettered words with a soft shadow that fade in as they are spoken. The key word of the line pops onto a solid rounded chip in bold marker lettering, and another word gets a loose ring drawn around it, as if circled with a pen. Chunks never overlap, text always stays inside the frame and off the face, and the footage is never touched.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every caption line with its chip and ring words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, pick a lane that keeps your face clear, choose chip and ring words, and build without overlaps or clipped text.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, lanes, typography, motion) and 8 moments, from the chest line to the closing chip.
