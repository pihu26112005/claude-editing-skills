## The style

A classroom chalkboard come to life. White chalk circles draw themselves around the speaker's head while a yellow sketchy title is written, then filled. Handwritten captions appear word by word, with the spoken word on a little dark tag and the key word in yellow.

Dry charcoal brush strokes slash across the screen to change scenes. The footage then sits in a card with a wobbly yellow chalk border on the board, with yellow step numbers for lists. Cream paper strokes sweep in for the close: a big sketchy quote with yellow quote marks and a doodle, the speaker still talking in a chalk card below.

Everything is drawn, written or brushed on; nothing slides or bounces.
