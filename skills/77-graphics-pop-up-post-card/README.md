# 77 · Pop-up Post Card

## Results

**Your content, animated by Claude.** A generic social post card on black with a name, handle, one-line post and engagement counts; the demo is a trail-notice post with a teal like burst.

![Pop-up Post Card demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pihu26112005/motion-graphics-skills-media/main/gifs/77-graphics-pop-up-post-card.gif)

**Format:** 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 7.27 s

## The graphic

A white card rises from the bottom of a black stage. The name and handle wipe in, the post text lands word by word, and the reply and share counts fade up. The like count ticks upward, then the like icon pops with a ring and a burst of dots. After a hold, everything leaves in reverse and the card drops away.

Perfect for engagement-style intros, post highlights, announcement quotes and social proof moments.

**Give it:**
- a display name and a handle (up to 20 characters each);
- your post text (up to 60 characters, one line);
- an avatar image (any photo or logo; it is cropped to a circle);
- reply, share and like counts;
- optional: accent colour (default pink-red), card, stage, text and meta colours.

**What you get back:** a finished 16:9 video (or a still image), rendered and checked by Claude.
