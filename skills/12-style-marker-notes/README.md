## The style
A creator-app look on top of your untouched footage. A thick yellow felt-tip marker stroke wipes in over a keyword. Captions build word by word, and a small yellow pill jumps to each word as it is spoken. A white to-do card lands, and ticks off a step in yellow when you say it. At the end a cream "camera roll" page rises and holds your live footage in rounded cards, with your other shots as small thumbs beside it. Motion is smooth and soft: quick wipes, short fades, no bounce.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.**

| Output | What you get |
|---|---|
| Final video / image | The finished file, built in code from your own footage and your exact words |
| Master prompt for Google Flow | One ready-to-paste prompt per clip, plus a caption table and an SRT |
