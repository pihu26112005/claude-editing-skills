## The style

A caption style built on tall, narrow capital letters. Each phrase pops up in full as thin white outlines, so viewers can read ahead, and every word snaps to solid white at the moment you say it. Phrases sit under your chin on speaker shots and move to the empty middle of B-roll shots, always as one to three tidy centred lines with a soft shadow. The footage stays full frame, with plain cuts and no cards or effects.

Perfect for talking-head explainers, tutorials, podcasts clips and any video where the viewer should follow the words.
