## The style
A caption style, not a layout: the footage stays full frame and untouched. Each phrase is a tight stack of two to four lines in the lower middle, and every line speaks in a different voice: heavy white capitals for the main words, a bright accent colour for the one payoff word, a large italic serif for the soft or turning word, and tiny plain connector words tucked against the baseline. Words pop up one by one as they are spoken, the stack holds still, and it cuts away in a single frame when the phrase ends.
