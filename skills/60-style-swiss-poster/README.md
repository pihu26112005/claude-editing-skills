## The style

Your video becomes a Swiss poster. Hairline grid lines draw across the picture with tiny dots at every crossing, then a flat Swiss-red block rises from the bottom and locks your footage into the top of the page. Captions are enormous, tight, bold sans-serif words stacked flush left, one word in a contrasting colour. On every cut a thin black line sweeps across and the page re-lays itself: warm paper with the picture bleeding off the right edge, or red with a tall picture and a giant white step number. A single flat red circle sits on a picture corner, a tiny progress line counts the shots, and the last line fills the page at its biggest.

Perfect for finance, design, education, listicles and any talking-head reel that wants a clean, loud, printed-poster feel.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every grid and layout move, exact caption lines with times and keyword colours, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, pick the layout that keeps your face in its module, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, grid, layouts, typography, motion) and 10 moments, from the grid build-in to the giant closer.
