## The style
Your footage fills the whole frame and plays live. Captions run along the lower third one or two words at a time: each new word fades in softly in pale lavender while the word before it turns white, and everything glows. Near the start, a stacked title in the same lavender appears small at the top, then snaps up to double size while a few white lightning cracks flicker across the edges. At every shot change the picture zooms, blurs, folds into a mirrored reflection with a red and blue edge split, and pulls back into sharp focus. On long shots, small zoom-in punches keep things moving.
