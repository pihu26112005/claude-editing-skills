## The style

A caption style with two voices. Above the head, one chunky condensed capital word with a thick black outline pops in for the key idea of each phrase, holds about a second, and fades. Below, the full sentence builds word by word in white handwritten-style script with a thin black outline, tucked into the lower left. The footage stays full-frame and untouched. Perfect for creator, vlog, coaching and talking-head videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, the crown words and script lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |
