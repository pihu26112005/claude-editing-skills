## The style

A hand-made zine on powder-blue paper. A cloud-edged paper band snaps over the top of the footage with one giant heavy black word and a tiny handwritten note, scattered with thin doodles. On cuts the word swaps, or two tilted photo cards with pale blue borders slide in over the live footage, showing the user's own earlier shots. Captions are small, slanted, spaced-out white handwriting, fading in word by word. The ending turns the speaker into a cloud-shaped window on the blue paper, under a fat white word.

Motion is snappy but never bouncy: paper snaps or slides, cards ease in, nothing glows.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every paper, card and word change, what each card shows, exact caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, check safe zones for the header and cards, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, shapes, typography, motion) and 11 moments measured from a reference video, from the cloud header to the cloud window page.
