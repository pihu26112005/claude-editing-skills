## The graphic

A plain search page fills a grey screen shot from very close, with a visible grid of green and magenta pixels and slight colour fringes on edges. The camera starts wide, zooms into the rounded search bar while your query types in, and holds with a blinking caret. It then pans fast, with motion blur, along the bar to the magnifier icon at its right end, and a cursor glides in to point at it.

Perfect for keyword hooks, topic reveals, "what people ask" intros and search-themed titles.
