## The style

A caption style with a fashion-magazine feel. Small white serif words appear one by one on the beat, and the key word of every phrase towers beside them in tall, stretched, high-contrast capitals that sharpen out of a pixel mosaic in a quarter of a second. Display words alternate white and one accent colour, bend into arches, waves and slants, and sometimes slide behind the speaker's head for depth. The last word lands giant and mixed case over the chest. Phrases leave with a hard cut and the footage stays full frame and untouched.

Perfect for talking-head reels, fashion, lifestyle, creator and storytelling videos.
