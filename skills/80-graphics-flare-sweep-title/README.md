## The graphic

A bold headline types on letter by letter over a dark, softly lit stage. A glowing smear and a sunburst flare with dark spokes chase the newest letter, then settle into crisp text. After a short hold the flare returns at the middle of the headline and the text fades away.

Perfect for title cards, channel openers, product names, hooks and chapter titles.
