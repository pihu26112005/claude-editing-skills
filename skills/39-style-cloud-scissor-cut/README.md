## The style

Your video turns into a DIY craft page. A powder-blue torn-paper cloud covered in pencil doodles drops from the top edge and a heavy black title pops onto it. Captions appear word by word in small slanted hand lettering. Then three paper circles float up, a frosted paper band rises across the bottom of the frame (the footage stays clear above it), a small hand with black scissors snips once, and bold white block captions fill the band. When the section ends the band falls away, and the cloud can return for a closing phrase.

Perfect for DIY, crafts, how-to, tutorials, creator tips and "easy steps" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, each cloud, paper and scissors beat, exact caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep every speaking face clear, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, paper shapes, doodles, typography, motion) and 8 moments measured from a reference video, from the cloud title drop to the closing cloud return.
