## The style

Your video turns into a soft, friendly product explainer. The opening words sit in a stack of frosted pills that sharpen from pixel blocks behind your head. A row of tiles ticks off, a white card draws a growing chart, switches flip on in a settings card, and the footage shrinks into a rounded window on a tinted gradient page where a glowing tube forks into two branches. Captions are small white lines with one accent word, and the last line lands as a giant glowing title under your chin.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds an interface-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every widget and what it does, exact caption lines with times and accent words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, pills, cards, window, pipes, typography, motion) and 11 moments, from the pixel-resolve pill stack to the glowing closing title.
