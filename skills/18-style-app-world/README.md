## The style

Your video turns into a polished product demo. A phone slides in beside you showing an app page made from your own shots. A frosted glass checklist ticks off each step as you say it. A clean white app window drops in, fills with thumbnails of your clips and types out your line before a soft pink send button pulses. On the payoff, the room fades to a dark plum app screen while you stay in front of a countdown and a filling progress bar. Captions are bold white two-liners with one soft-pink word.

Perfect for tech, SaaS, creator-tool, productivity and "how I work" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds an interface-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every interface piece and what it says, exact caption lines with times and pink words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan zones that keep your face clear, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, glass and white cards, phone, typography, motion) and 7 moments, from the phone mock-up to the dark app screen.
