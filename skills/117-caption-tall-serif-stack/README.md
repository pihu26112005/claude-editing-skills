## The style

A magazine-cover caption style. Key words from your script appear as stacked capitals in tall, narrow, high-contrast serif letters in one bold colour, centred above the speaker's head, with a soft light shimmer drifting through them. The block cuts in and out with no animation and swaps to the next phrase every second or so. Underneath it, a tiny bold white serif caption builds one word at a time just under the block. The footage stays full frame and untouched.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds this caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every title and caption line with timing, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan lanes that keep faces clear, chunk captions, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, title block, type, motion) and 10 moments, from the stacked title cut-in to the quiet close.
