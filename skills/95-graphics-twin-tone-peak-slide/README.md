## The graphic

A glass orb on a soft gradient dome slides in over black. A glowing area chart zigzags up to a single peak and a thin marker drops from it. The chart and the orb then glide past each other, the area beside the marker fills in a second colour as it falls, and a two-word label rises into place above the peak.

Perfect for record highs, turning points, market or traffic peaks, and short vertical data teasers.
