# 159 · Painted Portrait

**Step 1 of Motion Photos.** Turns an ordinary photo into a dramatic, textured oil painting of the same people and pose: thick impasto strokes, a refined face, smoky painterly background and moody chiaroscuro colour. The result is the input of [160 · Living Painting](../160-photo-living-painting/), which brings it to life.

**Format:** image · same aspect as your photo (vertical works best)

**Give it:** your photo; optional colour mood, aspect ratio and details to keep sharp.

**What you get back:** a filled painting prompt for any image model (Gemini or Google Flow image, ChatGPT image, Midjourney, Firefly), or the painted image when Claude has an image tool.

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md): the painting prompt and how Claude fills and checks it.
- [description.md](description.md): the inputs.
