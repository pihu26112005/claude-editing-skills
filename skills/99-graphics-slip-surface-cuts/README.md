## The graphic

A ragged-edged paper label sits in the middle of the frame. Its text types on letter by letter. Every 0.2 s the surface behind it hard-cuts to a new look: folded graph paper, wood grain, crumpled grid paper, a green cutting mat. The label swaps between a strong colour, a pale paper and a kraft tone, and a few scenes carry small props. The text stays put throughout.

Perfect for title cards, channel intros, topic reveals and quick brand stingers.
