# 109 · Shattered Front Page Pullback

## Results

**Your content, animated by Claude.** A newspaper front page that breaks into pieces and reassembles while the camera pulls back from a giant headline; the demo is a "Night Trams Roll Again" story under a teal tag.

## The graphic

The video opens on a giant two-line headline filling the square, printed on cream newsprint over a grey underlay. The page is cut into nine ragged pieces. They drift apart and tilt, then slide back together while the camera pulls out. Masthead, tag, subhead, columns and photo come into view, and the page settles on a faint newsprint background with thin cracks left between the pieces.

Perfect for breaking-news intros, announcement cards, headline reveals, "it all comes together" openers and story teasers.

**Give it:**
- a masthead (up to 22 characters), a two-line headline (up to 12 characters per line) and a subhead (up to 90 characters);
- 1 photo (any aspect; it is cropped and turned sepia);
- optional: edition tag, dateline, photo caption, body text, tag colour, paper colour, ink colour, underlay grey.

**What you get back:** a finished 1:1 video (or a still image), rendered and checked by Claude.
