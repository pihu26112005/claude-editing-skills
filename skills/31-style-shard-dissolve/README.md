## The style

A hot vermilion-orange, high-energy cut. Your video opens already sliced by slanted orange slats under a heavy yellow title. Shots arrive behind sliding bars or through a drift of grain. In the middle, the picture shrinks into an orange frame while a square card shows another of your shots and a giant outlined word creeps along the bottom. Every caption mixes thin white words with heavy slanted keywords. The last line ends as your picture crumbles into white specks and a pale wall of giant repeated words.

Perfect for fitness, sports, motivation, coaching, productivity and any punchy, energetic talking-head or B-roll video.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every slat, frame, card and caption, exact caption lines with times and heavy words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear of slats and cards, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, slats, inset window, typography, motion) and 10 moments measured from a reference video, from the slat opener to the word wall closer.
