## The style

A caption style with a hero move. On a talking-head line, a glossy curved sash sweeps in and orbits you: its upper half slips behind your head carrying the first words in tracked capitals, its lower half crosses your chest with the rest. Everything else is spoken on a small dark rounded plate with a thin accent border, where the words brighten one by one and one key word takes the accent colour. The footage is never touched.

Perfect for creator, coaching, product-launch and "here is the secret" talking-head reels.

**Give it:**

- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
