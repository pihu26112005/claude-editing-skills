## The style

A caption style that reads like a tiny poster. The footage stays full-frame and clean. In the lower third, small wide-spaced white capitals appear word by word, and one giant white italic serif keyword fades in beneath them, with a couple of caps words tucked under its tail. Phrases without a strong word become a single larger caps line. The last keyword can take one accent colour. A soft shadow keeps everything readable, and blocks change with hard cuts.

Perfect for talking-head, founder, finance, lifestyle and editorial videos that want captions with taste.
