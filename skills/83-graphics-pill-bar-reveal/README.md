## The graphic

A small round bubble with a magnifier rises on a soft gradient. It stretches sideways into a rounded search bar while the magnifier tilts upright, and your words fade in one by one. The bar swells slightly, then shrinks and fades away.

Perfect for search-style intros, "how to" and question titles, app promos and topic openers.
