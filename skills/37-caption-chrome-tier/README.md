## The style

A modern, glossy caption style. Connecting words are small, thin and white. The one word that matters in each phrase is huge, heavy and slanted, filled with a teal-and-silver chrome shine. Each phrase builds word by word and the next one flips to the other side of the frame, so the text zigzags down the lower half while the footage underneath stays clean and full-frame. The opening words sit as a compact headline above the speaker.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every caption line with times and keywords, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep your face clear, choose keywords and sides, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (chrome gradient, two-tier type, motion, safe zones) and 10 moments, from the tiered line to the end hold.
