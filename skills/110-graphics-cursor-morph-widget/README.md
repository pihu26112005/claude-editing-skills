## The graphic

A pill button on a warm grey canvas is clicked and shrinks into a loader, then a check, then a dynamic-island pill, then a music player. The cursor scrubs the progress bar, the card becomes a volume slider that stretches when dragged past its end, then a toggle that flips on the beat. The knob turns into a liquid tab indicator, the tabs open into a chart that draws itself with a hover tooltip, and the chart collapses into a command palette. Typing filters it, Enter fires a toast, and the toast settles back into the button so the video loops.

Every state is the same element changing size, radius and colour on springs, never cut. Text swaps with a short blur.

Perfect for product teasers, app launch loops, UI-motion portfolios and "everything is code" showreels.
