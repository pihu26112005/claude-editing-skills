## The style

Your video looks like it was doodled in a notebook. The opening word is scratched across the top in chalky block capitals three times, with you standing in front of it. A wobbly yellow marker line draws itself around you and keeps trembling. Captions are small handwritten words that pop in one by one, the newest on a dark label and one key word in yellow. At a cut, a jagged glitch edge sweeps down and your clip drops into a yellow-framed card on a charcoal chalkboard or a cream paper page with squiggles, a smiley, a cloud and a tilted photo of you.

Perfect for creator, lifestyle, education, journaling and "how I do it" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every title, doodle and board, exact caption lines with times and yellow words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan safe zones that keep your face clear, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, framed cards, boards, handwriting, motion) and 11 moments, from the chalk title stack to the card jolt.
