## The graphic

Three headlines in worn serif capitals are stacked on crumpled grey-green paper. They are too long for the frame, so the endings are hidden. The middle line is crisp and speckled with ink splatter; the top and bottom lines are soft and out of focus. The whole stack then jumps left in five hard, ticker-style steps and holds on the endings.

Perfect for title sequences, chapter openers, list intros and old-document or archive-style teasers.
