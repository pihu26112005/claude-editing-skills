## The style

A fashion-magazine caption style. Your footage stays full frame and untouched. Small bold lowercase captions in pale butter-yellow Didone type pop in word by word near the bottom, with one CAPITAL keyword per phrase. At the big words, a giant yellow capital version of the spoken word slams across the top of the frame, then everything cuts away at once, leaving clean footage between phrases. No fades, no boxes, no second colour. It is a caption style: it adds type only.

Perfect for talking heads, interviews, creator and lifestyle videos, storytelling and quote reels.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the caption style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every giant word and caption line with times and capital words, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, find a face-safe band for the giant words, chunk your captions, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, Didone typography, lanes, motion) and 8 moments measured from a reference video, from the giant keyword slam to the clean close.
