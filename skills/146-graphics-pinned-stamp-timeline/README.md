# 146 · Pinned Stamp Timeline

## Results

**Your content, animated by Claude.** Three dated picture stamps pinned on a scrolling paper ruler with counted gaps; the demo uses three flight firsts (balloon, biplane, jet airliner) with procedural pictures.

## The graphic

A paper-coloured ruler scrolls from date to date. Each event lands as a torn-border picture stamp with a caption and date tape, pinned to the ruler by a dashed line, and a tape label counts the years between events. The camera pulls back to the whole ruler under a torn headline stack, and a bracket measures the gap. A dark scene follows with a taped note card that lists the three events, then a horizontal ruler with the three stamps and two gap brackets.

Perfect for history comparisons, "closer than you think" reveals, product or company milestones and any date-gap explainer.

**Give it:**
- 3 events: year, name (up to 26 characters) and date tag each;
- 3 pictures (any photos; or let Claude draw simple ones);
- a 3-line headline, a 2-line note headline and an opening chip text;
- optional: kicker, closing chip, accent, tape, paper and dark colours (defaults measured).
