## The graphic

A rounded label with diagonal stripes and bold italic text rises from the bottom of a dark violet frame and glows. It tears in two along a zigzag crack between its words. The left half tilts away and flashes into a new colour, then a dashed leader line loops out of it and a callout types on above. The camera pushes in to the right half, which flashes into its own colour and gets its own leader and callout. The camera pulls back to hold both halves.

Perfect for before and after titles, two-part concepts, feature pairs, "this versus that" intros and software tutorial labels.
