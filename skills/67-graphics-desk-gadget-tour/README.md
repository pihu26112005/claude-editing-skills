## The graphic

A small handheld gadget lies on grid paper among desk props. Your title types onto its screen while the camera zooms in and tilts. The camera then spins one full turn around the desk, and a two-line headline types out on the paper next to the gadget. The camera drifts and holds.

Perfect for app and product teasers, vertical intros, launch announcements and "one-line promise" openers.

**Give it:**
- the screen text (up to 14 characters);
- a two-line headline (line 1 up to 22 characters, line 2 up to 20);
- optional: footer text, edge label, gadget colour (default charcoal), paper and text colour, grid off, props off.
