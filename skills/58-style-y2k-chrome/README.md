## The style

The early-2000s internet in icy blue. Your topic words drop in as shiny liquid-chrome bubble letters over a speaker framed like a printed poster. When the story cuts to B-roll, each shot floats inside a glossy old-computer window with a chrome title bar, on a sky-blue page of drifting light and twinkling four-point stars. Steps stack as greyed windows behind the current one, a small snapshot of the speaker pins to a corner, a pastel loading bar fills for time phrases, and a star flares open on every cut. Captions are holographic pills that shimmer pink, lilac and cyan, with one bold violet word, and the last words close in giant chrome with a burst of sparkles.

Perfect for lifestyle, fashion, music, creator, tech-nostalgia and "how I do it" videos that want a shiny, playful Y2K feel.

**Give it:**
- a video + its captions → it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image + text → a poster in this style;
- just a script → it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every window, sparkle and chrome word with its text, exact caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, plan face-safe zones, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (palette, window chassis, chrome lettering, pills, motion) and 13 moments, from the chrome title to the chrome closer.
