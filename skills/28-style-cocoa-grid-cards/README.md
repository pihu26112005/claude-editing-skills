## The style

Your video lands on a warm cream graph-paper page. It rises in as a rounded card under an italic title, with thin guide lines above and below. Captions type into a brown label bar, word by word. The card then swells to fill the frame. On a key phrase the picture slides away to reveal a dark cocoa page, where a white tag types a topic word and the line steps down the page in cream lettering. The footage glides back in and settles into a card again.

Perfect for explainers, education, finance, architecture and design, and calm "how I think about it" videos.

**Give it:**
- a video plus its captions, and it adds the style to your video without changing the footage;
- an image plus text, and it makes a poster in this style;
- just a script, and it asks about your story, then writes a Flow master prompt or builds a typography-only video.

**Choose what you get back.** Once Claude has everything it needs, it asks one question:

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Final video / image** | Claude edits and renders it, then checks the frames before handing it over |
| **Master prompt for Google Flow** | One detailed prompt per clip: a scene map with timestamps, every page, card move and tag, exact caption lines with times, the allowed-words list and things to avoid. Plus an SRT backup |

**What's inside:**
- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) tells Claude how to read your input, keep faces clear of cards and bars, find hook points, and place moments without breaking your video.
- [moments.md](moments.md) holds the style bible (cream and cocoa pages, rounded cards, label bar, type, motion) and 12 moments, from the grid page opener to the end hold.
