## The graphic

A rough, dry-brush colour bar sweeps across dark, grainy film while your title types on inside it. A thin line tag draws in at the top, camera-gate marks sit along the top and bottom edges, and scratches, dust and flicker keep the film alive. The bar's brush edge shifts shape every few frames, then the card holds.

Perfect for teaser titles, question hooks, gritty intros and punchy highlight lines.
